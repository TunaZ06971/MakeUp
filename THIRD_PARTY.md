# Third-party assets and local image decoding

- Canonical face geometry: MediaPipe, Apache-2.0. Original attribution is in `scripts/canonical/NOTICE`; full license is in `LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt`. Derived coordinate/topology tables in the Web and Apple source remain subject to that upstream license.
- Face Landmarker model/runtime: MediaPipe, served from this app's own origin. Setup is in `web/scripts/setup-mediapipe.mjs`.
- HEIC fallback: `heic-to` 1.5.2, LGPL-3.0, https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to/tree/f37af866f9aa6212ddc84b67a279c9f2386aba4f . Source, build instructions and license are also available from https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to . It includes libheif, https://github.com/strukturag/libheif . The decoder is unmodified and loaded as a separate JavaScript chunk only for HEIC files. Keep these notices with distributions and make the corresponding decoder source available under its upstream license.
- Material presets in `shared/materials.json` are authored for this application. They are visual approximations, not measured optical properties of the listed retail products.

The source photo, decoded pixels, masks and landmarks are processed locally. The HEIC decoder does not use a conversion service.

- 3D Web viewer/export: Three.js (MIT); source dependency pinned by `web/package-lock.json`.
- The scan topology uses all 898 faces from MediaPipe's canonical OBJ, including appearance surfaces at eyes/mouth. It is separate from the older makeup-only topology.
- Worker loader adaptations generated by `setup-mediapipe.mjs`: add a module-visible `custom_dbg` function and assign `ModuleFactory` to `globalThis`. The upstream WASM/model weights are not modified. Keep the upstream notices with these adapters.
- iPhone acquisition uses Apple's ARKit APIs and the runtime's personal face mesh. No third-party commercial head reconstruction API or raw TrueDepth export was added.

- Local `.makeupcapture` ZIP packaging: fflate 0.8.3 (MIT), https://github.com/101arrowz/fflate . Captured media are stored without re-encoding.
